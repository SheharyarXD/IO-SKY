## Findings that hold regardless of classification

* Document 1 states two different version numbers (1.3 on the cover and 1.0 in every footer, header and the control table), and refers to "Appendix I" that has been merged. The Product Owner's Document 2 acknowledges this. Treat v1.3 as intended and ask for a clean re-issue before any acceptance depends on it.
* The tier names in code (free, growth, elite) conflict with Document 1 section 13.1 ("all paid, no free tier") and with the product names Operations, Cyber, Elite Scan.
* Document 1 section 4 says MFA is mandatory for all five roles; BR-014 says otherwise. This is a direct conflict and needs written reconciliation, as the document itself requires.
* Absence from the 155-item checklist does not exclude anything from the full SRS (it did not contain telephony), and presence in a 29 October document does not make it SRS.
