- Card surface: oklch(0.16 0.022 260 / 0.7) glass with 1px white/8 border.
- Orange accent: oklch(0.72 0.20 45) ≈ #FF6A1A. Reserved for: active state, CTA, highlighted words, icons, score deltas.
- Eyebrows: SMALL CAPS, tracked 0.2em, orange, weight 600.
- Body text colour: oklch(0.78 0.012 250).
- H1 uses bold display sans-serif (Inter SemiBold/Bold or similar geometric — NOT Fraunces). The image uses a clean geometric humanist sans-serif, very close to Inter / Space Grotesk Bold.
