# Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Version:** 1.0
**Effective from:** 2026-05-25
**Document kind:** `nda`
**Parties:** IO SKY B.V. ("Disclosing Party") and the recipient (developer, contractor, partner or vendor) who accepts this NDA in the IO SKY platform ("Receiving Party").

This NDA protects the confidential information that IO SKY shares with the Receiving Party in the course of the engagement.

---

## 1. Definition of confidential information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information, in any form, that the Disclosing Party shares with the Receiving Party or that the Receiving Party becomes aware of through the engagement, including:

* Source code, infrastructure topology, deployment procedures, secrets and access credentials.
* Database schemas, automation logic, AI prompts, model configurations and benchmark results.
* Client lists, lead pipelines, financial information, pricing strategies and roadmap plans.
* Designs, mock-ups, brand assets that have not been publicly released.
* Internal communications, audit logs and security incident details.

Confidential Information does not include information that:

a. was already lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure, with proof;
b. is or becomes public without breach of this NDA;
c. was lawfully obtained from a third party without confidentiality obligations;
d. is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information.

---

## 2. Obligations

The Receiving Party will:

1. Use Confidential Information **only** for the purpose of the engagement.
2. Limit access to personnel who have a strict need to know and who are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.
3. Apply security measures at least equivalent to those it uses for its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, and never less than reasonable industry standards.
4. Not copy, reverse-engineer, publish, or disclose Confidential Information to any third party.
5. Notify IO SKY immediately at security@io.sky if Confidential Information is lost, leaked, suspected of compromise or sought by legal process.

---

## 3. Compelled disclosure

If a court or competent authority orders disclosure of Confidential Information, the Receiving Party will, where lawful, give IO SKY prompt written notice and reasonable assistance to seek a protective order or to limit the disclosure to what is strictly required.

---

## 4. Term and survival

This NDA enters into force when accepted in the IO SKY platform and remains in force for the duration of the engagement and for **five (5) years** thereafter. Trade secrets remain protected for as long as they qualify as such under applicable law.

---

## 5. Return or destruction

Upon termination of the engagement or on written request, the Receiving Party will return or securely destroy all Confidential Information in its possession, including derivative materials, and confirm in writing that destruction is complete. Backup copies retained for legal-hold purposes remain protected by this NDA until they are deleted.

---

## 6. No licence

Nothing in this NDA grants the Receiving Party any licence, by implication or otherwise, to any intellectual-property right of IO SKY. All rights remain with IO SKY.

---

## 7. Remedies

The parties agree that breach of this NDA may cause IO SKY irreparable harm for which monetary damages may be inadequate. IO SKY is entitled to seek **injunctive relief** in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity, without the obligation to post a bond.

---

## 8. Governing law and jurisdiction

This NDA is governed by Dutch law and disputes will be brought before the competent courts of Amsterdam.

---

## 9. Acceptance and audit

Acceptance is captured in the Workspace at onboarding. The acceptance row in `agreement_acceptances` is the authoritative record. A printed copy can be generated by IO SKY for compliance purposes.
