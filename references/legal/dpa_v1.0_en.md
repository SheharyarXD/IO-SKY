# Data Processing Agreement (DPA) — Scaffold

**Version:** 1.0 (scaffold)
**Effective from:** 2026-05-25
**Document kind:** `dpa`
**Parties:** IO SKY B.V. ("Processor") and the IO SKY Client ("Controller").

This document is a scaffold of the Data Processing Agreement that IO SKY enters into with Clients who use the Platform to process personal data of their own end users or employees. A countersigned copy of the DPA, with Annexes I–III completed for the Client's specific deployment, is required before processing begins. Where a Client does not require a separate DPA, the published version on this page is deemed accepted by use of the paid services.

---

## 1. Subject matter and duration

The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller for the purpose of delivering the IO SKY Platform services subscribed to by the Controller. Processing continues for the duration of the Master Services Agreement and ends in accordance with the retention schedule in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

---

## 2. Nature and purpose of processing

The Processor processes personal data to:

* Authenticate Controller users and protect accounts.
* Deliver the AI Scan, Strategy Call booking, Client Portal, Developer Workspace and Admin Portal features the Controller has subscribed to.
* Send transactional notifications (booking confirmations, MFA codes, security alerts).
* Generate AI-assisted reports and recommendations on data submitted by the Controller.
* Maintain audit logs required by Section 9 of this DPA and applicable law.

---

## 3. Categories of data subjects and personal data

| Category of data subject | Categories of personal data |
|---|---|
| Controller employees and authorised users | Identity, contact, account, MFA factor metadata, usage and audit data |
| Controller end-clients (where applicable) | Whatever the Controller chooses to upload (CRM contacts, project documents, support messages) |
| Visitors of Controller-facing pages | IP address, user agent, cookie consent records |

---

## 4. Obligations of the Processor

The Processor will:

1. Process personal data **only on documented instructions** from the Controller, including with regard to international transfers.
2. Ensure that personnel authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality obligations.
3. Implement the technical and organisational measures listed in **Annex II** (security measures), including encryption in transit, role-based access control, MFA, audit logging, backup and disaster-recovery.
4. Take all measures required under Art. 32 GDPR.
5. Engage subprocessors only as listed in **Annex III** (subprocessor list) and notify the Controller of intended changes with at least 30 days' notice; allow the Controller to object on reasonable grounds.
6. Assist the Controller in fulfilling its obligations to respond to data-subject requests under Articles 15–22 GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, portability, objection).
7. Assist the Controller with security, breach notification, impact assessments and prior consultation under Articles 32–36 GDPR.
8. At the choice of the Controller, delete or return all personal data after the end of provision of services and delete existing copies, unless EU or Member-State law requires storage.
9. Make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow audits, including inspections, conducted by the Controller or another auditor mandated by the Controller (subject to reasonable confidentiality and security constraints).

---

## 5. Obligations of the Controller

The Controller warrants that:

* Its instructions to the Processor comply with applicable data-protection law.
* It has a lawful basis for the processing.
* It informs its data subjects in line with Articles 13–14 GDPR.
* It does not upload special-category data without an explicit written agreement.

---

## 6. International transfers

Where processing involves transfers outside the European Economic Area, the parties incorporate the European Commission's **Standard Contractual Clauses (Decision 2021/914), Module Two (controller to processor)** by reference. The choice of governing law in the SCCs is the Netherlands and the supervisory authority is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

---

## 7. Subprocessors

The current list of subprocessors and their roles is published at [`/legal/subprocessors`](/legal/subprocessors). Equivalent contractual obligations are flowed down to each subprocessor.

---

## 8. Personal-data breach

The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any case within **72 hours** of confirmation, of any personal-data breach affecting Controller data. The notification will include the information required by Art. 33(3) GDPR.

---

## 9. Audit logs and records of processing

The Processor maintains internal records of processing activities under Art. 30 GDPR and an append-only, hash-chained audit log replicated across multiple regions. Excerpts of the audit log relevant to the Controller's tenant are available on written request, subject to redaction of cross-tenant operational data.

---

## 10. Liability and indemnity

Liability under this DPA follows the limitation of liability set out in the Master Services Agreement or, in its absence, the limitation in the [Terms of Service](/terms). Both parties remain liable to data subjects in line with Art. 82 GDPR.

---

## 11. Term, termination and return of data

This DPA enters into force on the Effective Date and remains in force as long as the Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller. After termination the Processor will, at the Controller's choice, delete or return all personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (for example tax retention).

---
