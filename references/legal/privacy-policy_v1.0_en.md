# Privacy Policy

**Jurisdiction:** European Union (with GDPR / AVG primacy) and the Kingdom of the Netherlands.
**Controller:** IO SKY B.V. ("IO SKY", "we", "us", "our").
**Contact for privacy matters:** privacy@io.sky

This Privacy Policy explains how IO SKY collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data in connection with the IO SKY platform — including the public website, the **AI Scan** intake, the **Strategy Call** booking system, the **Client Portal**, the **Developer Workspace**, the **Admin Portal**, our automations, notifications, AI assistants and our cloud infrastructure.

It is written to satisfy the General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (**GDPR**), the Dutch Algemene Verordening Gegevensbescherming (**AVG**), and the ePrivacy rules implemented in Dutch law.

---

## 1. Who we are and your relationship with us

IO SKY operates intelligent operational infrastructure for organisations that want to centralise execution, automate decisions, and scale with auditable AI. We act as **data controller** for personal data we collect about visitors of our website, applicants for our AI Scan, prospects who book a Strategy Call, our clients and their authorised users, our developers and our employees. For data we process **on behalf of clients** (for example data clients upload to the Client Portal or to AI Scan submissions), IO SKY acts as **data processor** under a separate Data Processing Agreement.

If you are unsure which role applies to your data, contact privacy@io.sky and we will clarify in writing within 14 days.

## 4. Cookies and similar technologies

Our cookie banner offers three categories.

* **Functional** (always active) — needed to keep you logged in, to enforce MFA, to remember your language, to prevent CSRF and to render the consent banner itself.
* **Analytics** — used to measure aggregate platform health (page load times, error rates, route popularity). Off by default. You can switch them on or off at any time via the **Manage preferences** link in the footer.
* **Marketing** — used by approved partners to attribute campaign performance. Off by default and never enabled unless you explicitly accept.

We log every consent decision (timestamp, version of the cookie policy, IP, user agent) in the `cookie_consents` table for accountability under Art. 7(1) GDPR.

A detailed list of cookies and their lifetimes is available at [`/cookies`](/cookies).

## 5. AI processing disclosures

Several IO SKY surfaces use AI. We are transparent about how that works.

* The **AI Scan** sends the data you submit to a Large Language Model that we operate or proxy. The model returns a structured analysis and recommendations. The model is configured to not retain your data beyond the duration of a single inference call. We never use your AI Scan data to train foundation models without an explicit, separate written agreement.
* The **AI Operations Agent** in the Admin Portal summarises platform telemetry. It does not transmit client data outside our infrastructure.
* The **AI Agents & IVR** module may generate outbound or inbound voice interactions. Such calls are recorded only when the recipient is informed and consents at the start of the call.
* All AI outputs are clearly labelled and are advisory. They do not constitute legal, financial, medical, or other professional advice. A human review may still be required. See our [AI Disclaimer](/ai-disclaimer).

## 6. How long we keep data (retention)

| Data | Retention period | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Lifetime of the account + 12 months after deletion request | Service continuity, fraud prevention |
| Authentication and audit logs | 24 months minimum, 10 years for compliance-relevant events | Security, AVG Art. 5(2), tax law |
| AI Scan submissions and reports | 36 months from submission | Service continuity for clients |
| Booking data | 24 months from event date | Operational records |
| CRM and lead data | 36 months from last meaningful contact | Sales operations |
| Email and SMS delivery logs | 12 months | Anti-abuse and quality monitoring |
| Cookie consent records | 24 months minimum | Accountability under Art. 7(1) GDPR |
| Invoices and tax records | 7 years | Dutch tax law (AWR Art. 52) |

When the period ends, the data is either deleted or anonymised so that it can no longer be linked to you.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR / AVG you have the right to:

* **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
* **Rectify** data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
* **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten") subject to legal-retention obligations.
* **Restrict** processing while a dispute is being resolved.
* **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest, including profiling.
* **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
* **Withdraw consent** at any time (it does not affect lawfulness of past processing).
* **Lodge a complaint** with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@io.sky. We will respond within **30 days**, extendable by 60 days for complex requests.

## 9. Security measures

IO SKY treats security as a non-negotiable engineering discipline. We apply, among others:

* TLS 1.2+ for all transport, HSTS preload on production domains.
* AES-GCM envelope encryption for sensitive secrets (TOTP seeds, hashed phone numbers).
* Bcrypt cost 12 for every password.
* Multi-factor authentication enforceable by role (TOTP, SMS, recovery codes, trusted devices).
* Append-only, hash-chained audit log replicated across three regions with WORM bucket protection.
* Role-based access control with `userProcedure`, `clientProcedure`, `developerProcedure`, `adminProcedure` at the tRPC layer and scope-based isolation by `organizationId`.
* Per-IP and per-account rate limits on login, MFA challenge, AI Scan, booking and proposal endpoints.
* Documented incident response procedure with notification within 72 hours of confirmed breach (Art. 33 GDPR).
